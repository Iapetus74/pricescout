Product information When space and weight are concerns, the Amplify 900 Synthetic Sleeping Bag is the answer. Ideal for summer or spring backpacking and hiking with new Amplify (expand) technology in knee and hip area to minimize heat loss. With a comfort rating of 7°C and transition rating of 2°C, this is a great three season sleeping bag. It also features a reflective zip puller and internal pocket for valuables.
Comfort temperature rating 7°C
Weight 0.975000
Locally Made No

Fill Type 100g/m² Thermo-Shield

Features
  • Elastic(Amplify) stitching to minimize heat loss around hips and knees but gives you room when you need it
  • 2-way zipper with draft baffle
  • Illuminate layer sandwiched between the inner lining and outer layers - a durable silver coated fabric that aids the bag to retain body heat
  • Insulated with Thermo-Shield
  • Internal pocket for valuables
  • Incredibly small pack size
