Product information The Helio two person, 3 season tent is built-to-last. Being the most compact tent in the First Ascent range, it is specifically designed to fit in the lower compartment of a hiking pack or in a top case of an overland motorbike. Weighing only 2.45 kg, this tent is highly packable and easily transportable for couples in search of adventure. A single pole construction allows for a hassle-free and quick setup, while offering sturdy protection from adverse weather conditions. Reflective guy ropes are added for extra visibility at night. Enjoy a dry, protected and restful night's sleep in this 3 season hiking tent.
