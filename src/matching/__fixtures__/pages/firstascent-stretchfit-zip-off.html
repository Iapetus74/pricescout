Fit guide

See which fit matches this product

Each of our products are designed and shaped specifically for its intended use.As our products are technically designed, it is very important that the product fits you accordingly. We categorise our products into four general fit types.

Athletic Fit

Conforms to the body’s contours.

Slim Fit

Closer-fitting. Slim-fitting technical garments may be worn over baselayers and light midlayers.

Regular Fit

Neither slim nor oversized. Regular-fitting technical garments may be worn over heavier midlayers.

Relaxed Fit

Drapes loosely on the body and can be worn over other layers

Expedition Fit

A very loose fit that is designed to be worn over insulating jackets and baselayers.

How to measure
A CHEST

Measure around the fullest part, across chest points, keeping the tape horizontal.

B SLEEVE

With elbow bent, measure from the center (back) of neck to elbow and down to wrist.

C WAIST

Measure around the narrowest part (typically the small of your back and where your body bends side to side), keeping the tape horizontal.

D HIP

Measure around the fullest part of your hips keeping the tape horizontal.

E LEG IN-SEAM

Measure from the crotch to bottom of the leg.

Product information Built for versatility and movement, the Men's StretchFit Zip-Off Pants combine the flexibility of StretchFit fabric with the practicality of a zip-off design. Engineered for durability and comfort, they stretch in any direction, ensuring unrestricted movement while climbing or trekking. Easily convert them into shorts or zip halfway for added ventilation. Lightweight yet tough, these pants are perfect for tackling mountains, trails, and rugged terrains.
