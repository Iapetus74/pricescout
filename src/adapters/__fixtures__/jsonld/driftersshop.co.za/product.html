    Description

    Five generations in and the legacy of unstoppable comfort continues. We kept the beloved 35 mm x 30 mm stack height but tweaked the ZipFoam™ midsole to bring you a new design while keeping the plush, responsive ride Ultraventure is known for. From trail, to gravel, to road, the trusted Vibram® XS Trek outsole features an intentional lug pattern and depth built for optimal all-terrain performance. The mesh upper delivers our signature fit with increased durability, now updated with our universal gaiter system.

      Specifications

      • Weight: 285.5g
      • Heel to Toe Drop: 5mm
      • Stack Height :35mm (Heel) x 3omm (Forefoot) 
      • Gaiter Compatible: Yes
      • Rock Plate: No
      • Cushion: High
      • Underfoot Feel: Plush
      • Support: Neutral
