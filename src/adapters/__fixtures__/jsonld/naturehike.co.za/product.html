The Naturehike Aqua Shoe is a lightweight water shoe built for beach, river, and water-based activities. A highly elastic mesh upper breathes well and dries quickly, while drainage holes in the rubber sole prevent water pooling underfoot. Two adjustable straps allow a secure, customised fit across different foot shapes.

Best for
Beach walks, fishing, rowing, kloofing, and water-based activities — or as a lightweight second shoe on multi-day hikes where a packable water crossing option is needed.

Not ideal for
Primary hiking footwear on rough or technical terrain where ankle support and a stiffer sole are required.

Additional features
- Drainage holes in the sole allow water to escape quickly after submersion.
- Anti-slip rubber tread provides grip on wet rocks and surfaces.
- Highly elastic mesh upper stretches to accommodate different foot shapes
- Lightweight at ±299 g
