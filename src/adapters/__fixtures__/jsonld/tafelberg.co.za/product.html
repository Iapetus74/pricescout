    LG 42L Microwave Oven MS4295CIS
    • Description
    • Details

    Fast Cooking Time

    With 1200 Watt of power on its side, the LG 42L Microwave Oven will reduce cooking time for any dishes, whether you warming milk, making popcorn or making chicken.

    Anti-Bacterial Interior

    You do not need to worry about a build-up of bacteria in the interior of your microwave. The Anti-Bacterial EasyClean™ interior coating ensures that cleaning will be simple and convenient, only a few wipes are required to clean the microwave interior.

    LED Lighting

    The interior of the LG Microwave Oven lights up 3 times brighter, than conventional models, with a white LED lamp, allowing you to easily check on your food without opening the microwave.

    Cooking Functions

    Cooking functions of this LG microwave include:

    • Reheat
    • Soften
    • Melt
    • Proof & Warm
    • Inverter Defrost

    Stable Ring

    Unlike the conventional microwave, the LG 42L Microwave Oven has six support points which will prevent off-center items from spilling or tipping while the microwave plate is rotating.

    Dimensions

    The LG 42L Microwave Oven measures in at H 308mm x W 544mm x D 432mm.
