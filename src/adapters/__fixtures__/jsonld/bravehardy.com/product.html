Description

The Osprey Ultralight 30 Litre Stuff Duffel is a lightweight, packable bag that adds extra capacity when you need it. Take it on day trips while travelling or use it for souvenirs on the return journey.

Keep small essentials close in the secure front pocket, with a key clip and stretch side pocket for added organisation. An adjustable shoulder strap with a pad makes it comfortable to carry.

Made with bluesign® approved recycled fabrics, with DWR treatments made without PFAS.

Features
  • Packs away easily for travel and provides extra capacity when needed
  • 30L volume offers room for day trips or return-journey souvenirs
  • Adjustable shoulder strap with pad for comfortable carrying
  • Secure zippered front pocket keeps small essentials close
  • Stretch side pocket adds convenient storage
  • Key clip helps keep keys together
  • Recycled, bluesign® approved fabrics
  • DWR treatments made without PFAS

Specifications
  • Volume: 30L
  • Dimensions: 22H X 48W X 35D CM
  • Main fabric: bluesign® approved 100% recycled 40D high-tenacity nylon
  • Accent fabric: bluesign® approved 100% recycled 100D x 210D high-tenacity triple ripstop nylon
  • DWR treatment: Made without PFAS
